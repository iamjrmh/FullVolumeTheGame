I do not host, supply, index or scan your music, and I have no way of knowing what is in your library. If you do not have the right to a file, do not put it in a folder the game is watching.

Charts you make

A chart you make in FullVolumeCharter is yours. I claim no ownership of it and no licence over it, and the tool sends nothing anywhere.

Be aware of what a .fvchart file actually contains: the song's audio is packed inside it, along with the album art and any backdrop you added. Sharing one is therefore sharing the recording, with all the same rules that would apply to sending somebody the audio file on its own. That is your call and your responsibility.

If you publish charts somewhere, do not imply they are official or endorsed. They are yours.

Online rooms

The online rooms are provided as a convenience and as they are. There is no uptime promise, no guarantee a room will still be there tomorrow, and no support contract.

When you are in a room with other people:

  • Do not harass, threaten, abuse or hound anybody.
  • Do not use slurs, or pick on somebody for who they are.
  • Do not broadcast anything sexual involving children, or anything illegal, over voice or chat. Ever.
  • Do not deliberately ruin a room: no blasting noise down the microphone, no spamming chat, no impersonating other people.
  • Do not attempt to break, flood, exploit or take over a server.

Rooms are not moderated in real time, because there is one of me. If you are in a room that has gone bad, leave it and make a private one with a code. Persistent or serious abuse of the public rooms can and will get an address blocked, without notice and without appeal.

Anything you say over voice or chat is heard by everybody in the room, and I have no way of knowing what was said or getting it back for you.
