Marketplace / Verified charters

For charters

Put your charts in front of everyone.

Verified charters get every .fvchart in their Google Drive folder listed on the Marketplace, where anyone playing FullVolume can find it, grab it and sing it. Your files stay in your Drive. We just point people at them.

Step one

Lay your folder out like this

One shared folder for all your charts. Inside it, a folder for each artist. Inside that, each chart, named after the song and the artist.

  • Your charts shared: anyone with the link
  • Dixon Dallas
  • Something To Feel - Dixon Dallas.fvchart
  • The Killers
  • Mr. Brightside - The Killers.fvchart
  • Somebody Told Me - The Killers.fvchart
  1. Share the top folder in Google Drive with General access set to Anyone with the link.
  2. One folder per artist, named exactly as the artist is spelled on the chart.
  3. Name every chart Song - Artist.fvchart, the way FullVolumeCharter exports it.
  4. Upload the whole .fvchart, untouched. Players download exactly the file you put there.

The more charts, the better

A folder with plenty in it shows us your style, how you handle tricky lines and how much care goes into each one. So fill it up before you apply. That said, every application gets read, and everyone has a real chance here.

No charts yet?

Make them in FullVolumeCharter, the free charting tool built for this game, and export each song as a .fvchart. Stuck on something? Ask in the FullVolume Discord.

Step two

Apply

Takes a minute. We will get back to you on Discord.

Your username, not your display name. Find it under your name in Discord.

A long number that never changes, so we can still reach you if you rename yourself. In Discord, right-click your name and choose Copy User ID. If you do not see it, turn on Settings > Advanced > Developer Mode first.

What happens next.

  1. 1

    We open your folder

  2. 2

    Yes or not yet, you get an answer. A not yet is never a no forever.

  3. 3

    Your charts go live

    Once you are in, every chart in your folder lands on the Marketplace, and new ones follow within a day.
