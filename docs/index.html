What it is

A karaoke machine
for the songs you
already own.

Every karaoke game wants to sell you the music. FullVolume hasn't got any to sell. It reads the songs already sitting on your drive and turns them into a proper karaoke night: lyrics, pitch, scoring, stars, the lot.

And it listens properly. Hold a note and you watch the score climb; slide off it and you know about it straight away. Sing the whole thing an octave down if that's where your voice lives. It still counts.

It also looks like somewhere you'd actually want to sing. Every last bit of it is hand drawn. And if you don't fancy the room, drop in a picture or a video of your own and that's your stage instead.

Features

Everything's in the box.

The whole game, one installer, nothing phoning home.

It actually hears you

Land the note and it knows. Nail it dead centre and it really knows. Sing high, sing low, sing it an octave down - if it's the right note, you get the points.

Your library, not a store

Point it at your song folders and it takes it from there. Only songs somebody actually sings on make the list, so you never pick a track and find there's nothing to sing.

Line up a set list

Queue songs up while somebody else is still singing, so the night runs itself instead of stopping dead between every track.

Online, with voices

Name a room, send it to your mates, and sing together from wherever you are. Chat freely between songs, and hold your key mid-song when you want to be heard over it.

Make it your room

Drop a picture or a video into a folder and it turns up as a backdrop you can pick. Give the menus one and your songs another.

Tuned to your gear

One button and it sorts out your audio delay on its own. Or tap along for a few bars and let it work you out. Either way, everything lines up.
